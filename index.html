The Park

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

We keep it simple: full hookups, a spotless facility, fast Wi-Fi, and a view of the water. The park is family-operated and the bathrooms are open around the clock.

No. 01Connection

Park-wide Wi-Fi

Fast, reliable Wi-Fi reaches every site. Stream, work remotely, or video-call home without thinking about it.

No. 02Hookups

30 & 50 amp full hookups

Water, sewer, and electric at every site. Pull-through and back-in spots available; long rigs welcome.

No. 03Facility

Showers & laundry, 24/7

Two washers and dryers and spacious private showers — clean, well-kept, and open whenever you need them.

No. 04Outdoors

Reservoir access

Visible from many sites. Walk down for fishing, kayaking, or to watch the sun drop behind the water.

No. 05Pets

Pet-friendly

Dogs welcome — please keep them leashed and clean up. There's plenty of open space to stretch their legs.

No. 06Service

Owner on-site

Family-run since day one. If something isn't right, we'll know about it — and we'll fix it.

On the Water

A reservoir at the edge of your site.

Many of our sites look out onto the water. Walk down to the bank for a morning of fishing, drop a kayak in for a slow loop, or just sit and watch the light change. It's the kind of quiet that's hard to find — and it's right outside your door.

Bring a chair, a rod, and a thermos. We'll handle the rest.

  • Fishing & kayaking
  • Sunset views from many sites
  • Walking trails along the bank
  • 10 minutes to downtown
  • Tarleton State 7 min away
  • Dinosaur Valley nearby
